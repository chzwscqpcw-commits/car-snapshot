A £20 MOT when the legal maximum is £54.85 sounds like the kind of deal with a catch. So is it? The short answer: budget MOT deals are almost always genuine — but there's one thing worth understanding before you book.

## Why the test is the same at any price

Every MOT in the UK follows the same DVSA inspection manual. The tester works through an identical checklist — brakes, lights, tyres, suspension, emissions, structure — and records the result on the same government database whether you paid £20 or £54.85.

Crucially, a garage **cannot legally do a "lighter" test for less money**. There's no budget version of the MOT. The pass or fail you get from a cheap test carries exactly the same weight as one from the most expensive garage in town. So on thoroughness alone, a cheap MOT is not a compromise.

## So why are some MOTs so cheap?

If the test is identical and capped at £54.85, why would a garage charge less than half that? Three reasons:

### It's a loss-leader

This is the main one. The MOT gets your car through the door. The garage knows that a percentage of vehicles will fail, and that many of those owners will pay for the repairs there and then rather than shop around with a failure hanging over them. The real margin is in the repair work, not the £20 test.

### It builds a customer relationship

A driver who has a good experience with a cheap MOT often comes back for servicing, tyres, and future tests. The garage treats the first cheap MOT as marketing — cheaper than advertising and far better targeted.

### Volume and overheads

High-throughput test centres and council-run facilities can run on thinner margins per test because they do so many. A small independent with low overheads can do the same.

None of these are reasons to be suspicious. A cheap MOT from a competent garage is simply a competitive price, not a trick.

## The one thing to actually check

Here's the nuance. A £20 MOT is a brilliant deal **if your car passes**. If it fails, the saving can evaporate — because the garage that tested it cheaply is also the one quoting for the repairs, and you're now in a weak position to negotiate.

So when you're comparing budget MOTs, look at two numbers, not one:

- **The test fee** — the headline price. Capped at £54.85, often much less.
- **The repair labour rate** — what they charge per hour if it fails. This varies far more between garages than the test fee does, and it's where a "cheap" garage can quietly make its money back.

A £20 MOT with a £90/hour labour rate can work out dearer overall than a £45 MOT at a garage charging £55/hour, if any work is needed. Ask about the labour rate before you book, especially on an older car that's more likely to need something.

## Watch what's bundled

Some "free" or very cheap MOT offers are tied to a paid service or come with add-ons — a seasonal check, a health inspection — that nudge the total up. These extras are often genuinely free, but occasionally they're there to justify a bigger bill. Always confirm the all-in price and exactly what's included before you agree. ## How to book a cheap MOT with confidence

1. **Compare local prices.** Don't just grab the first deal you see — [compare cheap MOT prices near you](/cheap-mot) to find the genuinely cheapest local option, not just the most heavily advertised.
2. **Check the garage's reviews.** A cheap test from a well-reviewed garage is a great deal. A cheap test from one with a reputation for inventing faults is not.
3. **Ask the labour rate.** One question — "what's your hourly labour rate if it fails?" — tells you whether the bargain is real.
4. **Know your retest rights.** If it fails and you fix the items within 10 working days, you're often entitled to a free or reduced partial retest. More in [what to do if your car fails its MOT](/blog/what-to-do-if-car-fails-mot).

## The bottom line

Cheap MOT deals are worth it. The test is standardised, so you lose nothing on quality, and a £25 MOT is genuinely £25 well spent if the car passes. Just remember the test fee is only half the picture — check the repair labour rate too, and a budget MOT becomes a no-brainer rather than a gamble.
