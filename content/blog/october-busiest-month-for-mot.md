If you have ever left it late to book an MOT in autumn and found the good local slots already gone, you weren't unlucky. October is one of the busiest months of the year for MOT testing in the UK, and it catches thousands of drivers out the same way every year.

The reason is baked into how we buy cars — and once you understand it, the fix is simple. You can beat the queue entirely without losing a single day on your certificate.

## Why October gets so busy

The autumn rush comes down to the way new cars enter the road, and how the MOT cycle follows them.

- **The plate change drives it.** The UK changes number plates twice a year, in March and September. Those two months are consistently the biggest for new car registrations — often well above an average month — because buyers hold out for the new plate. September is one of the two peaks.
- **First MOTs land three years later.** A new car doesn't need an MOT until it turns three. So every big September cohort comes due for its first test the following September and October. That surge repeats every year, putting predictable pressure on garages each autumn.
- **Renewals cluster too.** Once a car is on that annual cycle, its MOT tends to fall in the same window year after year. Layer years of September and October first-tests and renewals on top of each other and you get a genuine bottleneck.

This is the autumn sibling of the spring rush. March is the other plate-change peak, so garages get slammed around March too — we cover that in [Spring MOT Rush: How to Beat the Queues](/blog/spring-mot-rush-beat-the-queues). If your car is on the September plate cycle, October is your pinch point.

## The one insight that beats the queue

Here's the part most drivers don't realise: **you can have your MOT done up to a month — 28 days — before it expires and keep exactly the same renewal date.**

The new certificate runs from your old expiry date, not the day of the test. So testing early costs you nothing. You don't lose any of your 12 months, and you skip the worst of the crush by getting in before everyone else leaves it to the deadline.

So if your MOT is due in late October, your window to test early opens in late September. Book then and you'll usually find calmer garages and more choice of slots. We go deeper on the timing in [Can you get your MOT done early?](/blog/can-you-get-mot-done-early).

## Book ahead — don't leave it to the last day

In peak autumn weeks, the good local slots fill fast. Popular garages can be booked out days or even a couple of weeks ahead, and the convenient after-work and Saturday times go first.

Leaving it to the last day is a gamble. If the only slots left are after your expiry date, you're stuck: driving on an expired MOT is illegal and can bring a fine of up to £1,000, and it can invalidate your insurance too. During September and October, a few days' lead time is usually all it takes to get the slot you actually want.
