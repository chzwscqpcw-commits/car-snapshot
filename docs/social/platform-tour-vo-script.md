# Platform Tour — voice-over script

For `docs/social/platform-tour.html` (new **VO-led walkthrough** template — TikTok-native).
**Total length: ~31s.** Pure VO over the animated screen tour (no face on camera).

## How to record it

- **Persona:** the *calm, knowledgeable guide* — the "knowledgeable mate who tells you
  straight" (brand guide §1). Warm, unhurried, quietly confident. **Not** hype, not a
  hard sell. Smile slightly while you read — it warms the tone.
- **Pace:** slow and clear (~150 wpm). Leave the marked beats — the animation needs the
  air. Land the numbers; let them breathe.
- **Accent/spelling:** British throughout ("number plate", "twelve-fifty", "MOT").
- **Mic:** record close, dry, no room echo. One clean take per line is fine — we'll
  drop each line onto its timecode. Aim to *finish* each line just before the next
  timecode so it sits inside its scene.
- **Read the £/numbers as words:** "eight-thousand-three-hundred", "twelve-fifty a day".

> Every line is also **burnt in on-screen** (same words), so muted viewers read exactly
> what you say. Keep the spoken line and the caption identical — don't ad-lib.

## Alt hooks (A/B the first 1s — TikTok lives or dies here)

Swap **line 1** only; keep the rest. Test which holds:
- A *(default, value-led):* "Every car in the UK has a record — and most of it, you can read for free."
- B *(curiosity):* "There's a free site that tells you everything about any UK car."
- C *(problem-led):* "Stop paying for car checks — this does most of it for nothing."

Keep all three within the brand: no fake urgency, no hype emojis, honest claim
("**most** of it" — a free check can't see finance/write-off; that's the *Free vs Paid*
post's job).
