CROSS-POSTING TO THE FACEBOOK PAGE (post once → both update)
-----------------------------------------------------------
LINK ONCE (permanent, account-level):
  Instagram app > Profile > ☰ > Settings and activity > Accounts Center >
  Add accounts > Add Facebook account > pick the Free Plate Check PAGE.
  (Works the same starting from the FB Page > Settings > Accounts Center.)
  - Instagram must be a Professional account (it is).
  - Link the Facebook PAGE, not a personal profile.

MAKE IT AUTOMATIC:
  Accounts Center > Connected experiences > "Sharing across profiles" >
  set IG reels/posts to share to the Facebook Page by default.

THE "SHARE TO FACEBOOK" TOGGLE — where it is / why it vanishes:
  - It ONLY appears on the FINAL SHARE SCREEN when you FIRST publish a Reel.
  - It does NOT appear on the "Edit info" screen of an ALREADY-POSTED reel —
    editing only changes caption/location/collaborators/accessibility.
  - The instagram.com WEB flow often hides the toggle. It's reliable in the
    MOBILE APP or in Meta Business Suite. Post from those to get it.

BEST TOOL FOR BOTH (desktop + scheduling):
  Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com) — publish one Reel to the FB
  Page AND Instagram at once, schedule ahead (great for batching the calendar),
  and manage both inboxes in one place.

GET AN ALREADY-LIVE IG REEL ONTO FACEBOOK (retro-fit):
  - Meta Business Suite > create a Page post/Reel > upload the same video; OR
  - IG mobile app > open your reel > ⋯ > "Share to Facebook" (if offered); OR
  - Facebook app > create a Reel on the Page > pull from linked Instagram.

NOTE: a cross-posted FB reel reuses the IG caption. Fine as-is; if you ever
want FB-specific wording, post it separately via Business Suite.
