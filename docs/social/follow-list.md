# Social Follow-List & Facebook-Group Playbook

A launch-day checklist of who to follow (TikTok/Instagram) and which Facebook
**Groups** to join, for Free Plate Check's social channels. Handles verified
June 2026 — spot-check before mass-following (handles drift; impersonators exist).

**Strategic split:** on TikTok & Instagram you **follow accounts** (algorithm
seeding + format study + engagement). On Facebook you **join Groups and help** —
following pages there does little. The value is in *studying and engaging*, not
vanity following.

~36 for racist/extremist content). Not a place for the brand. Answer ULEZ
questions *inside the neutral motoring groups* instead.

## The golden rule for Groups (same value-first ethos as the reactive scout)
**Answer the question first** → describe what the free tool does in plain words →
**only share a link if someone asks "where do I check that?"** Never first-post a
link; never repeat across posts (mods + members read that as spam). Owner-clubs
and money-groups are help-only — name the tool once if asked, no URL.

Universal opener example: *"The MOT-history mileage chart shows if the numbers
jump around — that's clocking. You can also see every advisory and the test
pass-rate."* Only then, if asked, point them to the free check.
